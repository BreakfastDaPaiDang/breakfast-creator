# Visible-mark policy

Detection and treatment are separate decisions.

## Allowed without mark alteration

- Preserve the mark.
- Obtain and replace the file with an authorized clean master.
- Reject the candidate and choose another asset.

## Alteration gate

Crop, mask, inpaint, or replace a visible mark only when both are recorded:

1. The asset has publication rights appropriate to the intended project.
2. The rights holder explicitly permits modifying that particular mark.

An `owned` asset still needs mark authorization when the mark belongs to a platform, stock provider, collaborator, or other party.

## Derivative rules

- Never overwrite the original representation.
- Record treatment, parameters, operator, time, input hash, and output hash.
- Reassess composition after crop or mask.
- Reject treatments that obscure attribution required by a license.
- Prefer an authorized clean master over inpainting.
